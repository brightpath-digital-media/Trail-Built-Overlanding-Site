FAQ

Many portable power stations accept input from a single 100‑W solar panel, but recharge speed depends on the station’s maximum solar input rating and battery capacity. Check the unit’s rated PV input voltage/current and the charge controller type; a 100‑W panel will generally recharge smaller batteries more slowly and will be insufficient for rapid recharging of large-capacity units. For faster recharge or heavier loads, consider matching panel arrays to the generator’s specified maximum input.
